Why Studio Exists
# ADR-001 — Why Studio Exists

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Creating content for P² currently requires manually creating TypeScript files, adding metadata, importing content, and organizing folders.

This workflow does not scale across multiple projects.

## Decision

Build Studio as an independent application responsible for creating, editing and publishing structured content.

Public websites will consume this content instead of manually maintained TSX files.

## Consequences

- Content becomes independent of presentation.
- Every future project can share the same publishing workflow.
- Studio becomes the foundation of the P² ecosystem.
